Press titles: Bangladesh Monitor, Dhaka Tribune, The Daily Star, Ajker Patrika

  • Mapped the fair-floor structure and booth allocation across all four principals
  • Ran social and PR around both events, before, during and after
  • Monthly monitoring of the placements through the year
  • Designed and placed the print ads across all four titles
  • Worked on the UI for both the B2B (trade/partner) and B2C (consumer booking) websites
  • Ran competitor analysis and kept the team current on travel and OTA industry trends
  • Agency & media partners: a media-buying agency pool, The Mediam Group (Google's official Bangladesh media sales rep), Grey, MindShare, Carrotcomm

Print tear-sheets (Bangladesh Monitor, Dhaka Tribune, Daily Star, Ajker Patrika) are held by the agency — pending collection, not yet in this log.
